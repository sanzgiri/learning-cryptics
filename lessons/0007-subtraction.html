Lesson 7 · Cryptic Crosswords

Subtraction

Containers split a word open and put something in. Subtraction does the opposite: it splits a word open and takes something out, leaving the answer behind.

The recipe

Per the Best For Puzzles tutorial, one or more letters are removed from a fodder word to leave the answer. The indicator tells you where the cut happens:

  • Removing the first letter(s) — beheaded, not started, first off.
  • Removing the middle letter(s) — heartless, hollow.
  • Removing the last letter(s) — incomplete, endless, not fully.
  • Removing both ends — without limits, lacking wings.

Worked example

"Small flame, beheaded, is a recreation area (4)"

"A recreation area" is the definition, at the end. "Small flame" is the fodder — SPARK. "Beheaded" means drop the first letter:

SPARK → PARK

Four letters, means "recreation area." Both promises kept.

Practice

Find the fodder, work out which letter(s) the indicator drops, and where.

"Crockery, incomplete, reveals a body part (4)"

Indicator is "incomplete" — drop the last letter of a word for "crockery".
Crockery is five letters: C-H-I-N-A. Remove the last letter.
Definition: body part. Indicator: incomplete. Fodder: CHINA → drop the last letter → CHIN.

"Yell, hollow, becomes closed (4)"

Indicator is "hollow" — drop the middle letter of a word for "yell".
Yell is five letters: S-H-O-U-T. Remove the middle one.
Definition: closed. Indicator: hollow. Fodder: SHOUT → drop the middle letter (O) → SHUT.

"Celebrities, lacking wings, become road surface (3)"

Indicator is "lacking wings" — drop the first AND last letters of a word for "celebrities".
Celebrities is five letters: S-T-A-R-S. Remove the first and last.
Definition: road surface. Indicator: lacking wings. Fodder: STARS → drop first and last letters → TAR.
