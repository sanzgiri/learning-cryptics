Lesson 8 · Cryptic Crosswords

Bits and Pieces

Every trick so far has operated on whole fodder words. Bits and pieces clues zoom in further: instead of a whole word, a single extracted letter — the first, middle, or last letter of some word — becomes one piece of a charade.

The recipe

Per the Best For Puzzles tutorial, positional phrases point at a single letter inside the word that follows them:

  • "Head of X" / "start of X" / "initially" → the first letter of X.
  • "Heart of X" → the middle letter of X.
  • "End of X" / "conclusion of X" → the last letter of X.

That single letter then gets glued to other parts, charade-style, exactly like Lesson 4 — except now one of the "parts" is just one letter instead of a whole word. When "initially" (or "at first") applies to several words in a row, it's shorthand for "take the first letter of each" — the letters read off in order spell the answer.

Worked example

"Head of state and many make an opening (4)"

"An opening" is the definition, at the end. "Head of state" → the first letter of STATE → S. "Many" → LOT (as in Lesson 4). Glue them:

S + LOT = SLOT

Four letters, means "opening" (a slot). Both promises kept.

Practice

Work out which single letter the positional phrase points to, then treat the rest like a charade.

"Heart of stone plus pig enclosure is unlocked (4)"

"Heart of" means the middle letter of the word that follows.
Heart of "stone" (S-T-O-N-E) is O. "Pig enclosure" is a 3-letter word: PEN.
Definition: unlocked. "Heart of stone" → middle letter of STONE → O. "Pig enclosure" → PEN. O + PEN = OPEN.

"End of land plus spirit makes an instrument (4)"

"End of" means the last letter of the word that follows.
End of "land" is D. "Spirit" (the drink) is a 3-letter word: RUM.
Definition: instrument. "End of land" → last letter of LAND → D. "Spirit" → RUM. D + RUM = DRUM.

"Sam often forgets appointments, initially — furniture (4)"

"Initially" signals first letters — read the first letter of each word in order.
Sam, Often, Forgets, Appointments — four first letters.
Definition: furniture. First letters of Sam often forgets appointments → SOFA.
