Anagram Clues

You've already got the core contract down: definition + wordplay, both pointing at the same answer, checked against the enumeration. Hidden words were the gentlest wordplay trick because you didn't need to rearrange anything — just read straight through. Anagrams are the next step up: now you actually shuffle letters.

The recipe

An anagram clue has the same three ingredients as a hidden-word clue, just with a different trick in the middle:

  • Definition — same as always, plain synonym, start or end of the clue.
  • Anagram indicator — a word suggesting disorder, movement, or change: things like broken, mixed, chaotic, dancing, wild, reformed, acting up, oddly, at sea. It tells you "the fodder next to me needs shuffling."
  • Fodder — the exact letters to be rearranged. Count them: the fodder must contain precisely the same letters as the answer, no more, no fewer. This is what makes an anagram solution provable rather than guessed — if the letters don't match exactly, it's the wrong answer, full stop.
Indicator words aren't a fixed list — setters are creative. Almost any word implying disorder, movement, or transformation can work. See the Best For Puzzles anagram indicator reference for a long list, grouped by theme (disorder, movement, alteration, mental state, phrases like "at sea" or "in a whirl").

Worked example

"Silent, rearranged, to pay attention (6)"

"To pay attention" is the definition, sitting at the end. "Rearranged" is the anagram indicator. That leaves "Silent" as the fodder — six letters to shuffle: S, I, L, E, N, T. Rearrange them and you get LISTEN — also six letters, and it means "pay attention." Letters match exactly, both promises kept.

S-I-L-E-N-T → L-I-S-T-E-N

Practice

Same fodder-letters-in, same-letters-out check applies to all three. Count before you commit.

"Planet, hater acting strangely (5)"

Indicator is acting strangely — the fodder to shuffle is "hater".
Five letters: H, A, T, E, R. Think of what's beneath your feet.
Definition: Planet. Indicator: acting strangely. Fodder: HATER → H,A,T,E,R rearranges to EARTH.

"Garden reformed into peril (6)"

Indicator is reformed — shuffle the letters of "garden".
Six letters: G, A, R, D, E, N. The definition is "peril".
Definition: peril. Indicator: reformed. Fodder: GARDEN → G,A,R,D,E,N rearranges to DANGER.

"Rock: onset gone haywire (5)"

Indicator is gone haywire — shuffle the letters of "onset".
Five letters: O, N, S, E, T. Think of a small rock.
Definition: Rock. Indicator: gone haywire. Fodder: ONSET → O,N,S,E,T rearranges to STONE.
