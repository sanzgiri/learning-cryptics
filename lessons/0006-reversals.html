Lesson 6 · Cryptic Crosswords

Reversals

Back to a single-word trick, same family as hidden words and anagrams: one part of the clue, one letter-level transformation, no combining. This time the transformation is the simplest one there is — read the word backwards.

The recipe

Per the Best For Puzzles tutorial, a reversal clue has three ingredients:

  • Definition — same as always, start or end of the clue.
  • Reversal indicator — a word suggesting turning or flipping: going round, turned, backward, reversed, overturned, sent back.
  • Fodder — a word or short synonym that, read backwards, spells the answer letter for letter.

Same exactness rule as anagrams: reverse it wrong by even one letter and it doesn't work. Read it right and it must match the enumeration exactly.

Worked example

"Celebrity, reversed, becomes rodents (4)"

"Rodents" is the definition, at the end. "Reversed" is the indicator. "Celebrity" is the fodder — STAR. Read it backwards:

S-T-A-R → R-A-T-S

Four letters, means "rodents." Both promises kept.

Practice

Find the fodder word, flip it letter by letter, check it against the enumeration.

"Snare made from number sent back (3)"

Indicator is "sent back" — reverse a word for "number".
The number is three letters: T-E-N.
Definition: Snare. Indicator: sent back. Fodder: TEN → reversed is NET.

"Cooking vessels: halt, reversed (4)"

Indicator is "reversed" — reverse a word for "halt".
Halt is four letters: S-T-O-P.
Definition: Cooking vessels. Indicator: reversed. Fodder: STOP → reversed is POTS.

"Thin drinking tube from blemishes, turned around (5)"

Indicator is "turned around" — reverse a word for "blemishes".
Blemishes is five letters: W-A-R-T-S.
Definition: Thin drinking tube. Indicator: turned around. Fodder: WARTS → reversed is STRAW.

Primary source

In a real grid, reversal indicators often differ between across and down clues (down clues favour words like "rising" or "held up," since "backward" for a vertical word means "upward") — a wrinkle worth noticing once you're solving a real puzzle grid rather than isolated clues.
