Lesson 11 · Cryptic Crosswords

Complex Clues

No new trick this time — a synthesis instead. Per the Best For Puzzles tutorial, "a complex clue will use two or more cryptic devices" — for example both a reversal and an anagram in the same clue. Everything you need is already in your toolkit from Lessons 1–9. The only new skill is noticing that a clue is doing more than one thing at once, and unwinding them in the right order.

The recipe

  • Spot the definition, same as always — start or end of the clue.
  • Spot every indicator in the wordplay half — you may see two now instead of one (an anagram indicator and a reversal indicator, say).
  • Work from the inside out — resolve the smallest piece first (an anagram, a reversal, an extracted letter), then glue or insert it exactly like a charade or container.

Worked example

"Guard sent back, plus queen, forms storage unit (6)"

"Storage unit" is the definition, at the end. This has two moves stacked: "Guard" is WARD; "sent back" reverses it to DRAW. "Queen" is a standard cryptic abbreviation, ER (from the Latin Elizabeth Regina). Glue the two pieces, charade-style:

DRAW + ER = DRAWER

Six letters, means "storage unit" (a drawer). Reversal (Lesson 6) plus charade (Lesson 4), stacked in one clue.

Practice

Each clue below stacks two tricks you already know. Identify both before you commit to an answer.

"Meat, oddly, beside the river controls wild animals (5)"

Two tricks: an anagram (signalled by "oddly"), then a charade piece for "the river".
Meat's letters rearrange to a 4-letter word meaning "docile"; "river" is a standard 1-letter abbreviation: R.
Definition: controls wild animals. "Meat, oddly" → anagram of MEAT → TAME. "The river" → R (standard abbreviation). TAME + R = TAMER.

"Crockery, incomplete, raised is a bar exercise (6)"

Two tricks: subtraction (signalled by "incomplete"), then a charade piece for "raised".
Crockery is five letters; drop the last one. "Raised" is a 2-letter word: UP.
Definition: bar exercise. "Crockery, incomplete" → CHINA minus last letter → CHIN. "Raised" → UP. CHIN + UP = CHIN-UP.
